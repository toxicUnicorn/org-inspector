Data & metadata tools for Salesforce, in Safari.

Getting started

  1. Open Safari › Settings › Extensions and turn on Org Inspector.
  2. Click Edit Websites (or the extension’s permissions) and set access to Allow on Every Website, or at least allow your Salesforce domains.
  3. Open any Salesforce org and click the Org Inspector arrow on the right edge of the page.

What it does

  • Inspect and edit any record’s fields with Show All Data.
  • Run SOQL queries and export results with Data Export.
  • Insert, update, and upsert records with Data Import.
  • Explore the REST and Tooling APIs, org limits, and metadata.
  • Browse field- and object-level metadata directly from the Salesforce UI.

Your data stays yours

Org Inspector reads your existing Salesforce session (the sid cookie) so it can call the same APIs you already have access to, on your behalf. It talks only to your own Salesforce org. It sends nothing to the developer or any third party — no analytics, no tracking, no telemetry. Query history and preferences are stored locally in your browser and can be cleared at any time.
